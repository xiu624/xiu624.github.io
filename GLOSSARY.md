
# Prims Studio Website

The public website of Prims Studio, a Munich-based music studio that fuses Chinese and Western music. It presents the studio, its concerts, and its recorded pieces in three languages.

## Organisation

**Prims Studio**:
The Munich-based music studio the site represents. Its slogan is "We live music".
_Avoid_: Prims, Prims Group, the band

**Musician**:
A member of Prims Studio, introduced with a biography on the About page.
_Avoid_: Artist, performer, member

## Content

**Event**:
A dated, located public performance or concert by Prims Studio, with a time, a place and often a poster and programme.
_Avoid_: Concert, show, gig

**Work**:
A recorded or filmed piece of music by Prims Studio, usually presented with a video. It has no time or place.
_Avoid_: Piece, track, song, release

**Programme**:
The list of pieces and composers performed at an Event.
_Avoid_: Setlist, repertoire

**Teaser**:
The thumbnail image and one-line excerpt that represent an Event or Work in a listing.
_Avoid_: Preview, card, tile

**Portal**:
The large banner area at the top (and optionally bottom) of a page, holding a headline and background image.
_Avoid_: Hero, header image, banner

**Featured Event**:
An Event flagged to appear in the Portal on the homepage.
_Avoid_: Highlighted event, pinned event

**Latest Event / Latest Work**:
The single most recent Event or Work of the current Locale, shown on the homepage.
_Avoid_: Newest, recent

## Pages

**Home**:
The landing page. Combines an About summary, the Latest Event and Latest Work, and a call to join.
_Avoid_: Start page, index

**About**:
The page describing Prims Studio's philosophy, music, home city and Musicians.
_Avoid_: Über uns (as a canonical name)

**Impressum**:
The legally required German provider notice.
_Avoid_: Imprint, legal notice

## Language

**Locale**:
One of the three languages the site is published in: German (`de`), English (`en`) or Chinese (`zh`). Every page and post exists once per Locale.
_Avoid_: Language version, translation

**Default Locale**:
The Locale served at the site root without a path prefix: German.
_Avoid_: Main language, primary language

**Locale Prefix**:
The path segment (`/en`, `/zh`) that places a page in a non-default Locale. The Default Locale has none.
_Avoid_: Language folder

**Label**:
A short, reusable interface string (such as "Learn more" or "Back to top") defined once per Locale.
_Avoid_: Translation key, string, i18n

**Menu**:
The ordered set of navigation entries (About, Event, Work and social links) defined once per Locale.
_Avoid_: Navigation, nav bar
